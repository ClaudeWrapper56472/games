Games

Play in the browser, or install to the home screen.

  • Nine Lives

    One cat per row, column and colour, none touching — always on a 10×10 board, with the deductions ramping from Easy to Expert. Nine lives and one score run across every level: a wrong cat costs a life, a clean level pays one back.

     ›

  • Cat Doku

    One cat per row, column and colour, none touching. Boards grow from 5×5 to 10×10 as you climb the ladder, three lives per level, and Easy, Medium or Hard picks where you join.

     ›

  • Blockfit

    Drop blocks onto an 8×8 board and fill a row or a column to clear it. Pieces cannot be turned, every level asks for one more line than the last, and two lines taken together are worth four times two taken one after the other.

     ›

  • Carousel

    Lift a run of same-coloured rings off a peg and set it down on a matching colour or an empty one, until no peg holds two colours. Boards grow from three colours across five pegs to ten, with one spare peg per level and nothing to lose.

     ›

  • Clear Out

    Tap a block and it slides the way its arrow points, leaving through a gate of its own colour — or stopping wherever it runs out of room, usually in somebody else’s lane. The ladder has no end: every board is generated as you play, and each shows a par, the fewest taps that clear it.

     ›

  • Crown Cascade

    Swipe jewels to line up three. Four in a line leaves a rocket, an L of five makes TNT, five in a line a light ball — across twenty-four hand-authored levels of crates, ice and chains, each with a goal and a move limit.

     ›

  • Decant

    Pour liquid between tubes — onto a matching colour, or into an empty one — until every tube holds a single colour or none. Colours climb from three to ten, and finishing without an undo or an extra tube is a clean run.

     ›

  • Depot Rush

    Tap a bus into a boarding bay and everyone waiting in its colour climbs aboard, but a bus only leaves the lot if the way ahead is clear. Twelve levels; you lose when all three bays are taken and nobody on the platform wants any of those buses.

     ›

  • Jade Match

    Mahjong solitaire: match free tiles in pairs and clear the board. A tile is free when nothing rests on top of it and one side is open. Six boards open in order, from 38 tiles in a single layer to the full 144.

     ›

  • Meadowbrook

    Sow fields, feed the harvest through the bakery, dairy and mills, and fill orders for coins and experience. Coins buy land and homes, residents and level together gate what you can build, and every timer keeps running while the app is closed.

     ›

  • Merge Sort

    Drag two of a kind together and they become one thing a tier up — salvage, coffee, fish, and the producers that pop them out. Sixty-three cells and an energy bar that fills a point every two minutes are the whole constraint, and the orders waiting along the bottom pay for nine harbour projects.

     ›

  • Trio Stack

    Turn a stack of cubes, take the ones that can slide free towards you, and clear them three of a kind in a tray of seven slots. Every stack is built to come apart, and the levels grow from a nine-cube pyramid with three icons.

     ›
